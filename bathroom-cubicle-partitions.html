Types of Bathroom Partitions You Need

If you are looking for a bathroom cubicle partition then you need the knowledge of its different types and materials. These partitions have three types and many materials. Let’s take a look at their types first.

1. Floor Attached Partitions - As the name suggests, these partitions are attached to the floor. But their height would not reach the ceiling, hence the name, floor-attached partitions.

 

2. Floor-to-Ceiling Partitions - Partitions that are attached both to the floor and the ceiling, which means they cover all heights, giving full privacy to the user. However, these partitions take up full space too.

 

3. Ceiling-Hung Partitions - Partitions that are not attached to the floor but instead to the ceiling are called ceiling-hung partitions. They are good for the design and aesthetic of toilet cubicles.

 

Now that we know the three different types of partitions, we should also learn about the different materials one should consider. However, the thing is every manufacturer has diverse materials to provide. Sometimes they would change the name of their materials to present them uniquely in front of the client. Therefore, here as we will discuss the materials, just keep in mind that they might not be the every kind the market presents.

Materials of Bathroom Partitions You Need

  • Solid Plastic Partition - High-density polyethylene is also known as solid plastic. This thermoplastic polymer is a material that softens in heat and becomes solid in cold.
  • Powder-Coated Steel - This material is steel that is coated with powder for corrosion resistance. That means its coating protects it from rust.
  • Stainless Steel - This is a material most commonly used because it has corrosion-resistant properties and is highly durable with a modern finish.
  • Phenolic Laminate - By impregnating layers of fabric and paper with phenolic resin this material is created. They are water-resistant, durable, have a solid color finish, and are expensive.
  • Laminate Partitions - Plywood cores that are covered with plastic laminate are called laminate partitions. These materials are not moisture resistant but they are affordable.
  • Solid Surface Partitions - Glass, wood, and other solid surface materials are also used for partitions. However, they can be costly but have a premium finish.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What is the total price for the installation of bathroom partitions?

The bathroom partition installation cost will depend on various factors, for example, Compute area available, quality of the product chosen, styles, dimensions, designs, colors, and many more.

Q2. How long is the installation of restroom partitions?

We come and measure your space one day after you call. After that, it should not be longer than a week or two before the partitions are installed.

Q3. What other considerations should be taken into account before bathroom partitioning?

Before partition installation, measure up your space; consider the budget; know what quality you desire; materials to purchase; and choose any particular ends or themes.

Q4. Does every bathroom need a partition?

Most residential bathrooms do not need partitions, but commercial bathrooms and public ones need partitions.

Q5. What type of partition is best for small bathrooms?

For small bathrooms, floor-attached partitions and ceiling-hung partitions are better than floor-to-ceiling partitions. Furthermore, make sure these partitions are not thick.
