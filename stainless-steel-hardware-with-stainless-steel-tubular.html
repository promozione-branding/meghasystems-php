SS Hardware Tubular Series

Simple yet contemporary is synonymous with Classique's Evergreen. The model comes with the most extensively used stainless steel hardwares coupled with sturdiness of high pressure 12mm Compact Laminate. It offers standard adjustable foot along with stainless steel top rail for providing extra strength.

Rest of the hardwares like the hinges, locks, door knobs and coat hooks are also provided in stainless steel. Hence, making it an elegant and ideal option for heavy traffic areas where functionality matters the most.

Hardware Assembly Details

The adjustable foot that supports the panels is made from stainless steel (Grade 304) and its base is anchored to the floor with nylon plugs, inserted into floor with stainless steel screws. Stainless steal U-channels are attached with the divider panel to the wall as well as the mid panel. There is a floor clearance of 150 mm. Stainless steel top rail with panel holder is used to fix the panels at the top thru panel holder and wall bracket. Stainless steel corner connectors are provided for imparting extra support to the restroom cubicle of corner unit system. Further, doors are incorporated with the noise absorbing tape to reduce the noise effect.

Note:

  • Evo model comes with stainless steel round and box type top rail (Grade 304) arrangement
  • On special demand overall height of Evo model restroom cubicle can be increased upto 2100 mm.
  • On special demand adjustable foot can be provided in SS grade - 316.
