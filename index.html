Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What is a toilet cubicle?

Toilet cubicles are the private enclosed areas within washrooms for people to go through their physical needs. Such enclosed areas have a small toilet within them, which we call a toilet cubicle.

Q2: What is the price of these cubicles?

Q4: How do I place an order?

To place an order you should directly call us at the number shown on our website. If you want customised products then we will come to measure your space. As for the already-made products, we will deliver them to your place within the week of your order.

Q5: What do you call a toilet cubicle?

Toilet cubicles are small round containers fixed in the wall or the floor of an enclosed private area within the washroom. These fixed, small, enclosed areas are called toilet cubicles.

Q6: What is the minimum size for a toilet cubicle?

The minimum size for a toilet cubicle usually is 800mm wide and 1500mm deep.

Q7: What is a toilet partition?

To separate two toilet cubicle spaces and let the people have their privacy, a toilet partition is used. These partitions divide the spaces between toilet cubicles.

Q8: What is the meaning of cubicle in bathroom?

With the change in time, people nowadays prefer having a cubicle in their bathroom. With this they can complete their physical needs then continue to have a shower. It is a space optimizing idea.

Q9: What is the difference between a cubicle and a stall toilet?

A toilet cubicle is not as private as a stall toilet, after all, stall toilets are enclosed areas, while toilet cubicles can be open too with only a partition for privacy.

Q10: What is the difference between a partition and a cubicle?

A cubicle is a space fixed in the wall where people complete their physical needs, however, what divides two cubicles to provide privacy are called partitions.

Q11: Which toilet cubicle is most used?

HPL (High-Pressure Laminate) toilet cubicles are the most popular as they provide the perfect medium of strength.

Q12: What is another name for a toilet cubicle?

It's also be called toilet cubicles, restroom partitions, bathroom stalls,toilet suites compartments booth and kiosk.

Q13: How to build a toilet cubicle?

Measure the space, install pilasters, attach panels, hang doors with hardware, align properly, and get professional installation from Megha Systems.
